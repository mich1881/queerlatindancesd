FAQs

You may transfer your credit(s) to another series by contacting us directly via email or in person. Please provide your details and the series you wish to transfer to, and we will assist you with the process.

4 weeks Salsa or Bachata ONLY Series = 4 credits. 4 weeks Salsa & Bachata Series = 8 credits. Your credits expire 60 days after first day of class OR are valid for another consecutive series cycle.

Yes, you can split your classes between seasons. Just let us know your schedule preferences, and we’ll do our best to accommodate you.

Our reduced-pricing option is designed to make our classes more accessible. Please contact us directly for detailed information on pricing and eligibility.

A workshop is typically a one-time event focused on a specific topic or skill, while a series consists of multiple classes over a set period, allowing for more in-depth learning and progression. Our classes focus on discussing the details of body movement, dance technique, and musicality, while our workshops focus on engaging and fun learning experiences to get a feel of what you can learn in our series while connecting with other dancers within the community.

At this time, we are unable to offer refunds. Social dance events and classes require advance planning and costs, which means all purchases are final. If you are unable to attend, you may transfer your spot to a friend or community member. Please contact us if you’d like help arranging a transfer.

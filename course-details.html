Series Overview

4 Week Series 1: Level 1 Introduction to Salsa On2 & Bachata

A fast-paced beginner class that is meant to be retaken so you don’t have to commit to 6–8 week long classes. The series cycles for new students and for students who are retaking the series to develop muscle memory and build a strong foundation in their basics. Each cycle of the series, is a slightly different variation of the material.

What's it like taking Salsa Series 1?

  • 1st time BASICS: Exposure to the basics, learn timing and rhythm, retain at least 30% of the material.
  • 2nd time retaking BODY MECHANICS: Do the basics on your own, retain an extra 30%, focus on body movement
  • 3rd time retaking STYLING: Develop muscle memory of the basics. Retain an extra 40%, successfully execute basic combos. Add styling

What's it like taking Bachata Series 1?

  • Bachata Series 1 is meant to be taken once or as many times as you need. Bachata basics are much easier to learn than Salsa due to simpler rhythm and timing and can get more complex as you progress due to hip and body movement.

Note: Retention and progress depend on your background in body movement and dance. Practicing outside of class is mandatory and recap videos will be provided. The homework (documentaries and reading material) is optional but highly recommended.
